# Gig policy

Never create or claim Gigs involving illegal conduct, credential theft, phishing, malware, fraud, spam, harassment, unauthorized access, or unsafe physical-world actions.

Authorized use of accounts and credentials can be part of legitimate work. Use access within its granted scope and share credentials only through an appropriate private channel. Keep them out of public titles, Requirements, and logs.

## Coordination

The public brief must describe work that can be claimed at the listed pay. Questions, coordination, and private source materials can be handled elsewhere.

The Bouncer declines listings that mainly solicit messages, applications, or leads, or require contact to learn the core scope or negotiate separate payment.

Titles and Requirements remain visible in Board History. Contact addresses included in them remain public too. A temporary contact address can protect your usual address.

## Rights and permissions

For work with material licensing, attribution or provenance requirements, verify that inputs may be shared and outputs reused as promised. Do not promise originality, exclusivity or rights you cannot substantiate; clarify or decline that requirement.
